### Definición
Framework de 4 tipos de media: Paid (anuncios), Earned (PR, backlinks, menciones), Shared (redes sociales, UGC), Owned (website, email, CRM). PESO asegura cobertura multicanal balanceada.

### Propósito Principal
Garantizar que estrategia incluya todos los 4 tipos de media. Evitar dependencia excesiva en uno. Maximizar reach total.

### Pilares Principales
1. **Paid**: SEM, Display, YouTube, Programmatic
2. **Earned**: Backlinks, PR mentions, reviews, awards
3. **Shared**: SMO, user-generated content, advocacy
4. **Owned**: Website, blog, email, CRM, app

### Aplicación Práctica
- Paid: 30% budget
- Earned: Strategic PR, link building
- Shared: Community, advocates, UGC
- Owned: Content production, email nurturing

### ROI Típico
+30% reach improvement, +25% general ROI
