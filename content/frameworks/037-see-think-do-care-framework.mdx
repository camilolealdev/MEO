### Definición
Content marketing basado en etapa de audience: See (aware, no searching), Think (searching, evaluating), Do (ready to buy), Care (ya compraron).

### Propósito Principal
Crear contenido apropiado para cada etapa de audience readiness.

### Pilares Principales
1. **See**: Awareness content (educational, trending)
2. **Think**: Consideration content (comparativas, webinars)
3. **Do**: Conversion content (product pages, CTAs)
4. **Care**: Retention content (updates, success stories)

### ROI Típico
+42% audience alignment
